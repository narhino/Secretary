## Emergency numbers
- **191** police · **1669** medical emergency / ambulance · **199** fire · **1155** tourist police
  (English-speaking)
- **1178** immigration call centre · **1584** Department of Land Transport

## Handy apps
- Grab / Bolt (rides, food), LINE (everyone uses it, including landlords and clinics), LINE MAN
  (delivery)
- DLT Smart Queue (driving-licence appointments)
- Your Thai bank's app (PromptPay QR payments everywhere)
- TDAC (arrival card, official site only)

## Rules the secretary follows for Thai admin
1. Check this folder first. Then verify anything time-sensitive (fees, documents, office practice)
   with a fresh web search, cite the source and update the "last checked" line.
2. For any procedure: write a checklist, set a due date for each document and add a reminder for the
   appointment (`[1d, 2h]`) and for documents that expire (e.g. medical certificates after 1 month).
3. After each visit, write down what the office actually asked for. That beats any guide.
