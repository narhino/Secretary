## Prep template (about 2 hours, makes 8–10 meals)
1. Rice cooker: jasmine or riceberry rice for 4–5 days (or sweet potato in the oven or air fryer).
2. Protein, two styles so you don't get bored: e.g. 1.5 kg chicken breast (half garlic-pepper, half
   gaprao-style), plus 10 boiled eggs.
3. Vegetables: stir-fried morning glory or kale with garlic, a tray of roasted pumpkin or broccoli.
4. Portion into boxes using your macros. Fridge for 3–4 days; freeze the rest.
5. Midweek top-up (Wed): cook one more protein batch if needed.

## Where to shop
- **Makro**: bulk chicken breast, eggs, rice, frozen vegetables (cheapest per kg).
- **Lotus's / Big C / Tops**: everyday shop; Tops and Villa Market for Greek yoghurt, oats and whey.
- **Fresh markets (talad)**: vegetables, herbs and fruit, cheapest and freshest in the morning.
- Online: Lazada, Shopee or the Makro app for whey, oats and bulk staples.

## High-protein fallbacks when you didn't prep
- **7-Eleven:** boiled eggs, chicken breast packs (in the chilled section), Greek yoghurt, high-protein
  milk drinks, onigiri / sandwiches as the carb side.
- **Street food that fits the macros:** khao man gai (ask for less rice or skin removed), gai yang +
  som tam + sticky rice, pad krapow gai with a fried egg (ask for less oil: "noi naman"), tom yum
  with prawns, grilled fish (pla pao).
- Watch out for: sugar in drinks (ask "wan noi" or "mai wan"), and fried dishes as daily staples.
