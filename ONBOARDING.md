# Onboarding: let me get to know you

Answer in any order and as briefly as you like, here in chat or on Discord. Skip anything you'd
rather not share. I fill `profile/me.md`, `goals/goals.md` and the fitness and nutrition files from
your answers, and I set up the reminders.

## 1. Basics
1. What should I call you? Age, nationality?
2. Where in Thailand do you live (city or district), and since when?
3. What do you do for work? Remote, local, a business? Usual working hours?
4. When do you usually wake up and go to sleep?

## 2. Visa & admin (most time-critical)
5. Visa type (DTV, Non-B, Non-O, ED, retirement, visa exemption…)?
6. Date of your last entry, and the "permitted to stay until" date on your stamp?
7. Have you done a 90-day report? When is the next one due? (A photo of the last receipt is perfect.)
8. Has your landlord done the TM.30 for your address? Do you have the receipt?
9. Passport expiry (month and year only)?
10. Any trips abroad planned in the next 6 months?
11. Driving licence: which licences do you hold now (home country car/motorbike, IDP)? Car, motorbike,
    or both in Thailand?
12. Anything else pending with Thai admin (bank account, work permit, lease renewal, bills, SIM)?

## 3. Fitness
13. How long have you been training? What does a typical week look like right now?
14. Which gym and which days and times work for you?
15. Current numbers for the main lifts (or the last ones you remember)?
16. Goal for the next 3 months: fat loss, muscle gain, recomposition, strength, sport?
17. Injuries or limits?

## 4. Nutrition
18. Height, weight, and roughly your waist size?
19. Foods you love or avoid, allergies, alcohol habits?
20. Kitchen setup: full kitchen, rice cooker, air fryer, or just a microwave?
21. Which day suits meal prep? Weekly food budget?

## 5. Goals & growth
22. Where do you want to be in 1 year, and in 3 years? (Health, money/career, life in Thailand,
    relationships, skills such as learning Thai.)
23. The 3 things that would make the biggest difference in the next 3 months?
24. What usually derails you, and how should I push you when it happens?

## 6. How I should work
25. Tone: gentle, direct, or drill sergeant?
26. Reminders: minimal, normal, or nag me until it's done?
27. Do you want a morning brief (what time?), an evening check-in, and a Sunday weekly review?
28. Discord: do you already have a server? Do you have a computer that's always on, or would you
    rent a small server? (This decides how I'm hosted. See docs/discord-setup.md.)
