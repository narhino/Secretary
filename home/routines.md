# Studio routines: keeping a small place easy to live in

_Small spaces get messy fast and recover fast. The goal is never a big clean, just a quick reset
every day. The secretary asks about the studio in the evening check-in (logged as `tidy`:
clean / ok / messy) and adjusts these routines when the data shows a pattern._

## Daily (10 minutes, evening)
- [ ] Dishes done or in the rack, nothing soaking overnight (ants arrive fast in Thailand)
- [ ] Counter and table wiped
- [ ] Clothes away or in the laundry bag; nothing on the bed or chair
- [ ] Rubbish out if it's food waste (heat + humidity = smell)
- [ ] Tomorrow's gym bag / work bag ready by the door

## Weekly
- [ ] Laundry: wash, dry and put away in the same day (with the Sunday meal prep, or Saturday)
- [ ] Bed sheets (every 1–2 weeks)
- [ ] Floor: sweep and mop
- [ ] Bathroom: quick clean of the toilet, sink and shower; squeegee so mould doesn't build up
- [ ] Fridge check before shopping: throw out what's off, plan around what's left
- [ ] Restock: drinking water, eggs, protein, fruit, toilet paper, dish soap

## Monthly
- [ ] Clean the AC filters (rinse, dry): better air, lower electricity bill
- [ ] Deep-clean the fridge and microwave
- [ ] Check bills: electricity and water (condo office or app), internet, phone top-up
- [ ] Declutter one area for 15 minutes: one bag of things to donate or throw away

## Every 3–6 months
- [ ] Professional AC service (cleaning + gas check)
- [ ] Mattress flip/rotate, pillows washed or replaced

## Rules of thumb for living alone
- **One-touch rule:** put things where they live the first time; no "for now" piles.
- **Cook once, eat three times:** meal prep beats daily decisions and delivery apps.
- **Landing zone** by the door for keys, wallet, helmet and mail.
- **Reset before rest:** the 10-minute reset happens before the phone and the bed.
- When the studio slips to "messy" two days in a row, the secretary suggests a 20-minute reset
  instead of a guilt trip.
