## 90-day report (TM.47)
- **Who:** anyone on a long-stay visa or extension who stays in Thailand **more than 90 days in a
  row**. It is only an address notification. It is not an extension and it is free if on time.
- **When:** the filing window is about **15 days before to 7 days after** the due date. Leaving Thailand
  resets the count. After you re-enter, the next report is due 90 days after the entry.
- **How:** in person at your immigration office, by registered mail (send at least 15 days early), via
  an agent, or online (Immigration Bureau TM47 portal, linked from immigration.go.th). Online filing is
  unreliable at times and some offices want the first report after an entry made in person, so
  start early.
- **Late:** a fine of about **2,000 THB**, more if you are caught without having reported.
- **After filing:** keep the receipt in your passport. The receipt prints the **next due date**. Send me
  a photo and I'll set the next reminder from it.
- Reminder template: `repeat: every 90d`, `notify_before: [15d, 7d, 2d, 0m]`.

## TM.30 (residence notification)
- The landlord, hotel or house owner must notify immigration within 24 hours of a foreigner
  starting to stay at the address. Many offices also expect a new one after you return from abroad.
- You often need the **TM.30 receipt** for other things: extensions, the residence certificate, the
  driving licence, opening a bank account. Ask your landlord or condo office for a copy and keep a
  photo of it here.

## Extension of stay
- Done at your local immigration office before your permitted-to-stay date. The standard fee is
  **1,900 THB**. Requirements depend on the visa type (bank statements, letters, photos, TM.30,
  forms TM.7 and so on). The secretary prepares a checklist for your case.
- **DTV (Destination Thailand Visa):** a 5-year multiple-entry visa. Each entry gives **180 days**,
  and you can extend **once per entry** by 180 days at immigration (1,900 THB). Officers have
  sometimes asked for extra proof such as funds or work documents. Staying 180 days or more in a
  calendar year can make you a **Thai tax resident**.

## Re-entry permit
- If you are on an **extension of stay** and leave Thailand without a re-entry permit, the
  extension is cancelled. Fees: about 1,000 THB (single) or 3,800 THB (multiple), at immigration
  or the airport.
- A multiple-entry visa such as the DTV gives a new stamp on each entry. Check whether a permit
  matters for your case before you travel.

## Travelling in and out
- **TDAC (Thailand Digital Arrival Card):** complete it online within the 3 days before arriving.
  Use the official site only (tdac.immigration.go.th). Copycat sites charge fees.
- After every trip abroad: new permitted-to-stay date, a new 90-day count, possibly a new TM.30.
  Send me a photo of the new stamp and I'll update all of these.

## Overstay
- About **500 THB per day**, capped at 20,000 THB, plus possible **entry bans** for longer
  overstays. Never overstay. The secretary pings you 60, 30, 14, 7 and 1 days before your
  permitted-to-stay date.
