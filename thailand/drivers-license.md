# Thai driving licence — plan and checklist

> Last checked: 2026-10-21, from third-party guides and forums (no official DLT page). The
> Department of Land Transport (DLT) office decides, and offices differ (for example, whether a DTV
> gets a 2-year or 5-year licence, and whether conversions need a practical test). Call or visit your
> office first. DLT hotline: **1584**.

## Is it worth it / am I eligible?
- Driving or riding with only a home-country licence isn't enough. Short-stay visitors need an
  International Driving Permit (IDP) that matches the vehicle type. Residents should get a Thai
  licence, and insurance claims can depend on it.
- Generally needs a **non-immigrant visa status**. Tourist entries or visa exemption are usually
  refused. For **DTV**, practice varies by office: many issue licences, some only the 2-year one.
  → **Step 0: confirm with your DLT office for your visa type.**

## Steps
1. **TM.30 receipt** from your landlord / condo office (needed for step 2).
2. **Residence certificate** (proof of address) from either:
   - the **immigration office**: usually needs the TM.30 receipt, a passport with copies, the lease,
     photos and a form; it can take a few days, or
   - your **embassy**: fees vary (some charge a few thousand baht).
3. **Medical certificate** from any clinic or hospital (about 100–300 THB). It is valid for **1 month**,
   so get it close to the appointment.
4. **Licences:** your home-country licence (car and/or motorbike) and an IDP if you have one, plus a
   certified translation if the licence isn't in English.
5. **E-learning:** complete the DLT online training module if your office requires it, and bring
   the certificate.
6. **Book an appointment:** use the **DLT Smart Queue** app. Bangkok offices are usually booked
   weeks ahead, and walk-ins are not guaranteed.
7. **At the DLT office:** bring originals plus signed copies of the passport (bio, visa, entry stamp)
   and of everything above. Expect:
   - physical tests: colour vision, reaction time (brake pedal), depth perception, peripheral vision
   - a training video or lecture
   - a **theory test** (often 50 questions, 45 correct to pass), which many offices now require even
     for conversions
   - a practical test if you have no valid foreign licence for that vehicle type
8. **Pay and receive:** reported fees are about 205 THB (car) and 105 THB (motorbike) for the first
   **2-year** licence.
9. **Renew** the 2-year licence before it expires (to a 5-year one where your visa allows). The
   secretary sets the reminder.
