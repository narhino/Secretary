## Default starter split (if you don't already follow a program): Upper / Lower, 4 days
| Day | Main lifts (sets × reps) | Accessories |

## Progression rules
- **Double progression:** when you hit the top of the rep range on all sets at RPE ≤ 8, add weight
  next time (+2.5 kg upper body, +5 kg lower body) and go back to the bottom of the range.
- Log every working set (`80x8@8`). The secretary checks the trend in estimated 1RM each week.
- **Deload** every 6–8 weeks, or after 2 sessions in a row that go backwards: same exercises,
  about 60% of the volume.
- Heat in Thailand: drink 500 ml of water with electrolytes before training. Train early or late
  if your gym is not air-conditioned.
